## Progressing Over 4 Weeks
- Week 1: Use 70 % of 1RM for main lifts, hit lower rep range.  
- Week 2: Add 2.5‑5 kg to each compound lift, keep reps same.  
- Week 3: Increase reps by 2 per set (stay within 8‑12 range).  
- Week 4: Deload – reduce load to 60 % and cut volume by 30 %.  
- Repeat cycle, aiming for a 5 % load increase each new 4‑week block.
