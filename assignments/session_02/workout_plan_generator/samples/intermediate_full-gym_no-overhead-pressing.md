## Your Plan
Upper/Lower split – fits a 4‑day week, hypertrophy focus, and avoids any overhead pressing.

| Day | Focus | Est. Time |
|-----|-------|-----------|
| Day 1 | Upper Push | 75 min |
| Day 2 | Lower Quad‑Dominant | 75 min |

## Progressing Over 4 Weeks
- Week 1: Start with listed loads; aim for RPE 7‑8.  
- Week 2: Add 2.5–5 lb (1–2 kg) to upper‑body lifts, 5 lb (2 kg) to lower‑body lifts.  
- Week 3: Increase rep range by 1–2 reps per set while keeping load constant.  
- Week 4: Deload – reduce load by 10 % and keep reps the same.  
- Repeat cycle, adjusting load based on RPE and progression.
