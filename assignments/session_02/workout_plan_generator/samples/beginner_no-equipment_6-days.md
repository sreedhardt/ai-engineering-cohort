## Progressing Over 4 Weeks
- Week 1: Use backpack with ~5 lb (books) weight; stop 2‑3 reps before failure.  
- Week 2: Add 2 lb to backpack or perform an extra rep per set (stay in 6‑12 range).  
- Week 3: Increase each exercise to 4 sets instead of 3, keeping reps same.  
- Week 4: Deload – keep backpack weight same but reduce sets to 2 or drop reps by 2 per set.  
- After week 4, repeat the cycle, aiming to add weight or a set each new 4‑week block.
